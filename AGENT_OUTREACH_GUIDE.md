## Follow-Up Strategy

### Initial Contact
- Email or phone call with sample package offer
- Reference specific property benefits
- Suggest 15-minute conversation

### Second Contact (if no response)
- Call directly 2-3 days after email
- Quick pitch: "$199 for 125x ROI"
- Ask if they're open to quick demo

### Demo Call
- Show sample package (use 23711 Redbark Dr example)
- Highlight ROI metrics
- Offer trial on one listing
- Provide package link/location

### Post-Acceptance
- Deliver complete property package
- Provide download links
- Offer implementation support
- Follow up on results after 30 days
